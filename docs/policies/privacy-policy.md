## What we collect

- **Contact messages.** The contact page sends us your name, email, phone (if you give it) and your message, by email
  through Shopify's contact form. We use them only to reply.
- **Planner answers and your shortlist** stay in your browser (the shortlist is kept in local storage on your device).
  Nothing you choose in the planner is sent to us.
- **Technical data.** Shopify, which hosts the store, sets the cookies it needs and may collect usage data under its own
  privacy policy (https://www.shopify.com/legal/privacy). With your analytics consent only, the site counts which
  buttons are seen and used; these counts contain no personal data.
- **Merchant links.** Links to merchants add our source tags (utm_source=theoutdoorestate, utm_medium=referral and the
  piece's name). Once you are on a merchant's site, its own privacy policy applies.

We do not sell your data.
