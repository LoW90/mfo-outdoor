# The Outdoor Estate (working name)

Affiliate planning guide for luxury outdoor kitchens, fire and warmth, outdoor living and connected outdoor products, powered by Many Faces Online. Audience: older, wealthy homeowners, strongest in the Sunbelt.

- `site/index.html` is the whole page (no build step beyond copying to `dist/`).
- Income model: affiliate commission only. Lead partner Kalamazoo Outdoor Gourmet (10%, 90-day cookie, Awin), then BBQGuys (about 4%, Impact), Beatbot (10%). No program has approved us, so links carry only `utm_source=theoutdoorestate`.
- Prices on the page were checked on the linked merchant pages on 2 November 2026; recheck before launch.
- Before launch: brand name and domain, business details, privacy policy and terms, licensed photography or films with real people for the hero, affiliate approvals (then swap links for tracking links), a GitHub repo (`LoW90/mfo-outdoor` suggested).
